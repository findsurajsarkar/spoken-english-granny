App link: https://findsurajsarkar.github.io/spoken-english-granny/

## Positioning (one line)

**Speak English without fear.** A kind AI granny listens, never laughs, and corrects you gently with a
red pen, in simple words, in English, Hinglish or Hindi.

Who it's for: shy learners, students, job seekers, working professionals and homemakers, mostly Hindi
speakers improving spoken English. The emotional hook: *nobody is watching; Granny is a safe place.*

What makes it different from other apps:
1. Red-pen notebook corrections that look like a real teacher checked your copy
2. Explanations with zero grammar jargon, in Hindi/Hinglish if you want
3. Warm, grandmother personality: encouragement, not judgement
4. Marks that reward harder sentences, not only fewer mistakes
5. ₹99/month, well below the usual ₹299+ for similar apps

## Getting real testimonials (the honest way)

Fake reviews are illegal in India (Consumer Protection rules on fake and paid reviews), break Play Store and
App Store policy, and can get the app removed. Collect real ones instead; they convert better anyway:

1. Give the app to 20–30 real people: friends, family, students and WhatsApp groups. The Play Store closed
   test needs 12+ testers anyway.
2. After a week, send a 3-question Google Form: *What did you like? What was confusing? May we quote you
   (name + city)?*
3. Add quotes **only from people who said yes** to `TESTIMONIALS` in `src/config.ts`. The "What learners
   say" section on the landing page appears automatically.
4. Screenshots of real WhatsApp messages (with permission, numbers hidden) also work well on Instagram.
